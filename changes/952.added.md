- `find_flask_routes` records gain `urls`, the URLs each route is served at. `find_flask_blueprints` records gain `url_prefixes`, each blueprint's served prefixes. Both are composed as Flask does: a `register_blueprint(..., url_prefix=...)` overrides the blueprint's own prefix, a nested blueprint adds its parent's, and a route's rule is joined to its blueprint's prefix. They are sorted lists, because a blueprint registered twice is served twice, and are present only when every registration's prefix is statically known. They are absent when a prefix isn't a string literal, the blueprint isn't registered in the searched scope, a registration has a subdomain or a `**` splat, or there's a cycle. `path` and `url_prefix` are unchanged. A blueprint route with the empty rule `""` (served at the prefix itself) is now listed too.
