- `python -m pyeye.docs_facts architecture plan|validate|report` reviews one area of a codebase against its recorded architecture: the `structure` kind's `arch.*` facts, the `CHECK no-import` rules on its pages, and the decision entries whose `Applies-to` sites fall in the area, resolved through pyeye (#1178).
  - `plan AREA` gives the area's packet. `CHECK` rules are already settled as adherence or deviation, and the decisions to confirm are candidates.
  - `validate` checks a reviewer's findings. Each is one of four outcomes (adherence, deviation, decision needed, ambiguity), and a deviation always names the rule, decision or convention it deviates from.
  - `report` ranks them: rule deviations, then decision deviations, decisions needed, ambiguities, and an adherence count.
  - A repository with no recorded architecture is told so, and nothing is guessed. The `architecture-review` skill moves onto it in a later change (#1132).
