- `python -m pyeye.docs_facts architecture` sorts the decision sites it can't place into three lists, `unresolved_sites`, `short_form_sites` and `external_sites` (#1178).
  - **`unresolved_sites`:** stale project references only.
  - **`short_form_sites`:** a name not qualified from the project's own top-level package, such as `edges.resolve_imports`, which is never resolved.
  - **`external_sites`:** a standard-library or installed name, which no area holds.
  - None of them is ever reported as "not in this area".
