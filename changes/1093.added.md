- The decision-log tooling now ships with pyeye: `python -m pyeye.decisions index`, `check` and `migrate` list and validate a repo's `docs/decisions/entries/`. The `decision-log` skill runs it through the plugin's environment, so it works in any repo, not just this one. `--decisions-dir` points it elsewhere, and `--grandfathered NAME` exempts older entries from the `Applies-to` rule. A repo with no decision log gets "no entries".
