- A docs-facts config can declare fact families **reference-only** (`reference_only = ["arch"]`): their facts stay out of the uncovered-fact ledger, and `generate` and `check` print a note naming them (#1108).
- A fact family can keep its **own snapshot file** (`[tool.pyeye.docs_facts.paths.snapshots]`, e.g. `arch = "docs/facts/arch.json"`), so a large family doesn't swamp `facts.json` diffs. The removed-name record and the changed-fact list read every snapshot file together, so moving a family removes nothing.
