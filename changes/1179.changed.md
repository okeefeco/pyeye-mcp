- The `architecture-review` skill now reviews one area against its recorded architecture, through `python -m pyeye.docs_facts architecture`: its `arch.*` facts, `CHECK` rules and decision entries (#1179).
  - Every finding is an adherence, a deviation (always naming the rule, decision or convention it deviates from), a decision needed or an ambiguity.
  - When the user resolves an item, the skill drafts a decision entry through `decision-log` and, where an existing rule kind can check it, a `CHECK no-import` line, so the next run reports adherence to it instead of asking again.
  - There are no dismissals, and a deferred item is simply reported again.
  - The old axis-and-grade audit, its auditor prompt and its reproduction gate are gone from the skill.
