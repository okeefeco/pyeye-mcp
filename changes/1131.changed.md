- The `decision-log` and `duplication-review` skills' plugin commands now stop with a hint when copied out of the plugin, instead of failing on `/.venv: Permission denied` (#1131).
- `decision-log` no longer assumes or creates `docs/decisions/` or a `DECISIONS.md`. It looks for an existing log, asks before starting one, and passes another location with `--decisions-dir`. It also says how to anchor a decision with no issue or PR, runs a gold `Verify` once before writing it, and its flow diagram now matches its steps (#1131).
- `duplication-review` warns that one dismissal covers every pair in a family, so a family of three or more sites can bundle a real copy with unrelated pairs (#1131).
