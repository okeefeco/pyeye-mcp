- `inspect` reports a function's, method's or class's `deprecation`: the static evidence that it is deprecated, as a list of `{kind, via, line}`. `"unconditional"` (a `@deprecated` decorator, or a `warnings.warn(..., <deprecation category>)` that runs on every call, in `__init__` for a class) means the symbol is deprecated. `"conditional"` (such a warning under a branch) usually deprecates one argument or path, not the symbol. `[]` means checked and nothing found; the field is absent when the definition couldn't be read. A category counts when its name ends in `DeprecationWarning` or its class statically subclasses one, as Django's `RemovedInDjango70Warning` does; `FutureWarning` doesn't (#1082).
