- `python -m pyeye.decisions check --resolve` resolves every handle in the `Anchor` and `Applies-to` fields of the active decision entries with pyeye, and fails on any that no longer resolve, with `entry:line`. A repo path in those fields must exist. A reference to the standard library or an installed package that doesn't resolve is listed, not failed. A short form that can't be resolved without a guess (`Class.method` with no package) fails in entries dated on or after the `short_forms_fail_from` date a repo sets; older ones are counted on every run. Superseded and amended entries are not checked. `--resolve` needs pyeye importable and fails without it; plain `check` stays standard-library only (#1182).
