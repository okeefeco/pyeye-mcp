- A re-export made by a relative import in a package's `__init__.py` (`from .thing import X`) now resolves to its definition (`pkg.thing.X`), like any other re-export. Before, `resolve`/`inspect("pkg.X")` answered `pkg.X` at the import line, though `inspect("pkg.thing.X")` listed `pkg.X` among its `re_exports` (#857).
