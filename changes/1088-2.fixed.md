- An overloaded function's `signature` in `inspect` is now its implementation's, matching its `parameters`, `return_type` and location; it was the first overload's. With only overloads (a `.pyi`, a Protocol) it is the first overload's. A `@deprecated` on a single overload is now reported as `conditional` deprecation evidence, and an overload-only definition no longer reports `deprecation: []` as if its body had been checked (#1088).
