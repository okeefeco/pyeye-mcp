- `expand(…, "superclasses")` says why each unresolved base didn't resolve. `unresolved_superclass_details` sits beside `unresolved_superclasses`, in the same order, as `{base, reason}`, with `reason` one of `not_a_name` (a call or other expression), `unresolvable`, `ambiguous` (bound to several classes, e.g. a try/except import shim), `not_a_class`, `restricted` or `no_handle`. An `ambiguous` entry adds `candidates`, the handles it could be, sorted, and `candidate_count`. A count above the list's length means some candidates can't be shown (#1065).
